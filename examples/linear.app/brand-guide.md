## Tone of voice

Calmly ambitious and technically fluent. Linear pairs a clear thesis about the future of product development with concrete workflow language, restrained confidence, and a deep concern for craftsmanship.

### Future-facing, grounded in systems

Frames AI as a structural change in product development, then explains the shared environment needed to make it work.

- **Do:** Connect statements about the AI era to specific changes in context, coordination, or execution.
- **Don't:** Promise an undefined AI revolution or present agents as magic.

> "AI increases what teams can create, but it also raises the bar for clarity and coordination." ([source](https://linear.app/about))
> "A purpose-built system where teams and agents operate together in a shared, structured environment." ([source](https://linear.app/about))

### Momentum without noise

Uses direct verbs and operational outcomes to describe speed as focused progress, not frantic activity.

- **Do:** Name what becomes faster and which friction is removed.
- **Don't:** Use breathless urgency, exclamation-heavy copy, or speed claims detached from quality.

> "Reduces noise and restores momentum to help teams ship with high velocity and focus." ([source](https://linear.app/))
> "Keep PRs moving without sacrificing quality." ([source](https://linear.app/))

### Craft-led conviction

Treats software quality as a deliberate practice, extending from foundations to small details.

- **Do:** Describe the care, decisions, and standards behind the work.
- **Don't:** Equate quality with generating more output or adding more features.

> "What unites us is relentless focus, fast execution, and a deep care for software craftsmanship." ([source](https://linear.app/about))
> "We are looking for people who share our passion for software craftsmanship and getting even the smallest details right." ([source](https://linear.app/careers))

### Direct and technically specific

Assumes a product-building audience and explains work through recognizable artifacts and actions.

- **Do:** Use precise workflow terms and get to the practical point.
- **Don't:** Replace concrete actions with vague business jargon or lengthy introductory framing.

> "Synthesize customer feedback, draft PRDs, and delegate issues." ([source](https://linear.app/enterprise))
> "Get to the point and assume good intentions." ([source](https://linear.app/careers))

**Tone spectrum** (0 to 100)

- Formal 35 Casual
- Serious 12 Playful
- Technical 32 Plain
- Understated 58 Bold

**Style rules**

- Use sentence case for headings; keep headings compact and centered on a capability or outcome.
- Pair short declarative headlines with explanatory sentences around 20–25 words; retain technical detail when it adds precision.
- Lead product copy with Linear, the system, or the action; use second person sparingly and first-person plural mainly for company perspectives.
- Use periods and restrained punctuation. Avoid routine exclamation marks and reserve questions for purposeful prompts.
- Use established product and engineering terms such as PRDs, PRs, triage, and code reviews rather than replacing them with vague abstractions.
- Write Linear with a capital L, not “Linear app”; capitalize dedicated releases as proper nouns and express numerical proof with exact figures.

**Words we use:** purpose-built, teams and agents, product development, shared context, planning and building, focus, ship, company priorities, software craftsmanship, quality, AI, triage

**Words we avoid:** magic bullet, game-changing, effortless, hustle, crush it, synergy, disrupt everything

**Measured:** 24 words per sentence, reading ease 33 (Dense (college level)), headings in Sentence case, "you" 0.6 per 100 words vs "we" 1.2.

## Messaging

- **Product and engineering teams:** Connect customer feedback, planning, issues, and code reviews to move from idea to launch with clarity and focus.
- **Enterprise product organizations:** Align teams and agents around company priorities, shared context, and structured workflows at scale.
- **Teams adopting AI agents:** Delegate work, automate product operations, and measure execution while keeping agents connected to team context.
- **Prospective teammates:** Build carefully crafted software within a remote team that values quality, principles, and focused execution.

### One system for teams and agents

Keep people and agents working from the same context, priorities, and structure.

> "It provides a shared product development system, where teams and agents work from the same context, priorities, and structure." ([source](https://linear.app/enterprise))
> "Customer feedback, decisions, and code all live in one place, so everyone works from the same context." ([source](https://linear.app/enterprise))

### From context to execution

Turn conversations, customer feedback, and plans into actionable work connected to product priorities.

> "Automatically turn conversations and customer feedback into actionable issues that are instantly routed, labeled, and prioritized for the right team." ([source](https://linear.app/))
> "AI coding accelerates individuals. Linear ensures their work is tied to team context and company priorities." ([source](https://linear.app/enterprise))

### Speed with focus and control

Remove operational friction so teams can move quickly without losing alignment or quality.

> "Triage, routing, labeling, and updates happen automatically, so teams can focus on building." ([source](https://linear.app/enterprise))
> "Streamline code reviews with clear diffs, better context, and fewer back-and-forth comments." ([source](https://linear.app/))

### Purpose-built craftsmanship

Build product tools around the practices of strong teams and a lasting commitment to software quality.

> "Linear is shaped by the practices and principles of world-class product teams." ([source](https://linear.app/))
> "A tool with unparalleled speed, focus, and design." ([source](https://linear.app/careers))
