## Tone of voice

Technically precise and commercially ambitious. Stripe connects programmable infrastructure to concrete business outcomes, supporting its confidence with scale, operational detail, and a broader belief in economic growth.

### Infrastructure-minded

Explains products as adaptable systems businesses can build on, rather than isolated features.

- **Do:** Name the underlying capability and explain how it connects to a customer's systems or workflows.
- **Don't:** Replace technical specifics with vague promises of effortless transformation.

> "Reliable, extensible infrastructure for every stack." ([source](https://stripe.com/))
> "Stripe is programmable financial infrastructure that helps you build and run a global business." ([source](https://stripe.com/products))

### Outcome-led

Leads with growth, saved resources, and practical business improvements.

- **Do:** Start with an action and its business benefit, then explain the enabling product.
- **Don't:** List features without showing what they help a business achieve.

> "Safeguard your business without slowing growth" ([source](https://stripe.com/products))
> "Move fast and free up resources with flexible billing models, including subscription management and one-time invoicing, tax, and automated reconciliation." ([source](https://stripe.com/products))

### Measured confidence

Makes ambitious claims with operational evidence rather than exuberant language.

- **Do:** Pair claims about scale or reliability with a number, time frame, or specific capability.
- **Don't:** Use exclamation-heavy hype or unsupported superlatives.

> "Businesses on Stripe generated $1.9T in 2025." ([source](https://stripe.com/))
> "Handle thousands of transactions per second with consistent speed and reliability, even during peak traffic periods." ([source](https://stripe.com/))

### First-principles ambition

Frames financial technology as a way to improve the conditions for economic growth.

- **Do:** Connect new capabilities to making businesses easier to start, scale, or serve globally.
- **Don't:** Treat innovation as novelty without an economic or practical purpose.

> "Stripe is a technology company focused on improving the conditions for economic growth and prosperity." ([source](https://stripe.com/about))
> "We don’t assume the best way to do something is the way it’s always been done." ([source](https://stripe.com/careers))

**Tone spectrum** (0 to 100)

- Formal 35 Casual
- Serious 8 Playful
- Technical 35 Plain
- Understated 65 Bold

**Style rules**

- Use sentence case for headings; favor short, benefit-led headings beginning with action verbs.
- Aim for sentences around 18 words, allowing longer sentences when explaining technical capabilities or related benefits.
- Address customers as “you” and “your”; use “we” and “our” to explain Stripe’s mission and what it builds.
- Use precise financial and developer terminology when useful, and connect it to a clear business outcome.
- Use em dashes for ranges and explanatory extensions; keep exclamation marks rare and questions mainly for FAQs.
- Use numerals, percentages, and compact scale notation such as $1.9T; retain dates, units, and qualifiers with numerical claims.

**Words we use:** infrastructure, programmable, economic growth, grow, start and scale, global, payments, revenue, flexible, integrate, financial tools, ambitious, developer tools, AI

**Words we avoid:** magic, miracle, get rich quick, crush it, mind-blowing, guaranteed success, one-size-fits-all

**Measured:** 17.6 words per sentence, reading ease 45 (Dense (college level)), headings in Sentence case, "you" 1.3 per 100 words vs "we" 1.6.

## Messaging

- **Founders and growing businesses:** Start accepting payments quickly and expand with tools that support new revenue models and global customers.
- **Enterprises:** Reliable infrastructure, custom pricing, and support for complex integrations and large payments volumes.
- **Developers and technical teams:** Programmable APIs, SDKs, documentation, and integration options that fit existing systems.
- **Platforms and marketplaces:** Embed financial products, coordinate payments and payouts, and monetize transactions.
- **AI businesses and agent builders:** Flexible billing, agentic checkout, and infrastructure that lets software transact.

### Infrastructure for business growth

Stripe helps businesses start, scale, and grow revenue with financial tools that work individually or together.

> "Grow your business with a comprehensive set of payments and financial tools⁠—⁠designed to work individually or together." ([source](https://stripe.com/))
> "We build programmable financial infrastructure, rethinking from first principles how financial services should work, to make it easier and cheaper for any business to start and scale." ([source](https://stripe.com/about))

Stripe helps businesses serve customers and move money across borders with global payments and financial capabilities.

> "Take payments anywhere—online, in person, and through agents. Access 125+ payment methods and 135+ currencies." ([source](https://stripe.com/products))
> "Process payments, store multiple currencies and stablecoins, and send and spend money globally." ([source](https://stripe.com/products))

### Reliable, adaptable foundations

Stripe gives businesses flexible integration paths and reliable infrastructure that can handle demanding transaction volumes.

> "Adapt Stripe to your business needs with flexible integration options." ([source](https://stripe.com/))
> "Handle thousands of transactions per second with consistent speed and reliability, even during peak traffic periods." ([source](https://stripe.com/))

### New models of commerce

Stripe enables businesses to monetize platforms, AI products, and emerging forms of financial services.

> "Monetize platform transactions—including payments, card interchange, and financing fees." ([source](https://stripe.com/))
> "Stripe is the economic infrastructure for AI." ([source](https://stripe.com/products))
