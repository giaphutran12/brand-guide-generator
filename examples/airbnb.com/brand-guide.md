## Tone of voice

The supplied copy is primarily careers-led: welcoming, creatively ambitious, and direct about flexibility and belonging. It pairs human invitations with concrete scale and practical qualifications.

### Belonging expressed personally

Addresses readers directly and frames inclusion as being welcomed, empowered, and able to contribute.

- **Do:** Use you and your to connect belonging with a concrete experience or opportunity.
- **Don't:** Describe people as interchangeable resources or make belonging sound like an exclusive privilege.

> "We welcome you" ([source](https://careers.airbnb.com))
> "We’re committed to ensuring Airbnb is a place where people of all backgrounds, identities, and experiences can thrive." ([source](https://careers.airbnb.com))

### Creatively ambitious

Uses expansive language about creative work and impact, supported by invitations to participate.

- **Do:** Pair an ambitious headline with a specific action, role, or collaborative opportunity.
- **Don't:** Rely on abstract innovation jargon without explaining what people can do.

> "Work at one of the most creative places on Earth" ([source](https://careers.airbnb.com))
> "Join our global creative community, where passion and collaboration drive innovation to make products that impact the world." ([source](https://careers.airbnb.com))

### Human flexibility with practical boundaries

Acknowledges individual needs while clearly qualifying what the company offers.

- **Do:** State the human benefit first, then include relevant limitations in plain language.
- **Don't:** Promise unrestricted freedom or hide qualifications behind legalistic phrasing.

> "We know there is no one-size-fits-all approach to work." ([source](https://careers.airbnb.com))
> "where regulations allow" ([source](https://careers.airbnb.com))

**Tone spectrum** (0 to 100)

- Formal 55 Casual
- Serious 25 Playful

- Understated 65 Bold

**Style rules**

- Use short, invitation-led headings; sentence case predominates, with title case retained for named phrases such as Live and Work Anywhere.
- Keep body sentences near the measured average of 19 words, using plain high-school-level language.
- Use we and our for the company, and you and your when addressing readers directly.
- Use contractions naturally but sparingly; avoid exclamation marks and rhetorical questions.
- Present scale with numerals, as in 2007, 1.5 billion, and 5 million; capitalize Hosts when referring to Airbnb Hosts.
- State practical qualifications explicitly rather than presenting benefits as unconditional.

**Words we use:** Airbnb, Hosts, connect, connection, belonging, creative, community, world, Live and Work Anywhere

**Words we avoid:** human capital, synergize, exclusive elite, disruptive ecosystem, unrestricted, guaranteed

**Measured:** 18.9 words per sentence, reading ease 51 (Plain (high-school level)), headings in Mixed, "you" 3 per 100 words vs "we" 3.4.

## Messaging

- **People looking for homes on Airbnb:** Find homes through Airbnb.
- **Prospective employees:** Explore open roles, workplace belonging, creative impact, and flexibility in where they live and work.
- **Media and editorial users:** Access media assets and understand restrictions on their editorial use.

### Connection starts with belonging

Airbnb presents a welcoming workplace as the starting point for creating connection and belonging in the world.

> "Creating connection and belonging in the world begins with a workplace where you're both welcomed and empowered to be your authentic self, so you can deliver your best work." ([source](https://careers.airbnb.com))

### Flexibility recognizes individual needs

Employees can live and work across the world where regulations allow, while retaining opportunities for in-person connection.

> "We know there is no one-size-fits-all approach to work." ([source](https://careers.airbnb.com))
> "opportunities to connect in person" ([source](https://careers.airbnb.com))

### Creative collaboration drives impact

Airbnb invites people to join a global creative community and collaborate on products that impact the world.

> "Join our global creative community, where passion and collaboration drive innovation to make products that impact the world." ([source](https://careers.airbnb.com))
> "Work alongside industry-leading talent and be part of our vibrant network, encompassing people from all walks of life." ([source](https://careers.airbnb.com))

**Proof points**
